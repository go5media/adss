About us

Advertising that supports responsible community CCTV.

UK Safety Camera Network Ltd (USCN) supports community safety through appropriately assessed CCTV operations. We also provide commercial CCTV installation, repairs, modernisation and maintenance.

USCN Ads gives local advertisers the opportunity to appear on qualifying physical signage. Revenue contributes to sustaining the community network and its supporting infrastructure. Operational decisions remain needs-led, lawful and separate from commercial influence.

Public safety and privacy remain our priority.

Advertising does not provide access to CCTV footage, personal data or decisions about where cameras are installed. Our surveillance activities follow separate governance and data-protection safeguards.

Our purpose

Supporting safer communities through responsible CCTV.

Advertising helps fund a network that must meet real operational needs. Camera installations and replacement signage are considered on safety, necessity and operational grounds—not to manufacture advertising opportunities.

Why USCN Ads

Practical benefits, clear expectations.

Our approach is based on a defined quantity of genuine physical signage inclusions rather than a conventional timed advertising campaign.

01

No fixed advertising expiry

Your purchased inclusions are not sold as a one-, three- or six-month campaign. After 18 months from contract formation, you may request a refund for any unfulfilled prepaid inclusions at the original purchase price.

02

Review before taking payment

USCN evaluates advertiser suitability, existing commitments and realistic opportunities for new or replacement signage before deciding whether to offer a paid booking.

03

Location-specific visibility

Choose a town and a specific advertising position. A qualifying inclusion is fulfilled only when an appropriately branded sign is physically installed at a genuine operational site.

04

A community-safety connection

Your advertising helps support USCN's public-safety mission without buying influence over CCTV operations or access to personal information.

05

Optional QR code

Add a QR destination to help people visit your website. Managed redirects are subject to USCN's separate Redirect Service Policy and fair-use arrangements.

06

A straightforward enquiry

Select 1–50 signs and view an indicative total before sending any details. Joining the interest list is free and does not reserve an allocation.

How it works

From enquiry to a formal offer.

01 / EXPRESS INTEREST

Tell us what you're looking for

Choose a town, position, QR preference and sign quantity. Add your brand details and preferred start date. The enquiry opens in your own email application for you to send.

02 / SUITABILITY & CAPACITY

We assess your request

We review suitability, existing allocations and realistic new or replacement sign opportunities, ordinarily using an 18-month planning horizon. That assessment is not a guarantee of installation by a particular date.

03 / OFFER & PAYMENT

Make an informed decision

If we can offer an appropriate opportunity, we send you terms and an invoice. No paid allocation arises unless the final offer is expressly accepted and payment has cleared.
